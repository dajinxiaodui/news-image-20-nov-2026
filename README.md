A pure-stdlib Python program that draws one newspaper front page summing up Friday 20 November 2026: the World Court ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, Trump, Xi and Putin signing a peace declaration at their first three-way summit in Shenzhen, the Dow falling 1,101 points for a sixth straight down day while Anthropic's Nasdaq debut closed 17% up, a Houthi missile damaging the Saudi sovereign wealth fund tower in Riyadh, two tankers hit by mines and on fire in the Strait of Hormuz, the COP31 draft dropping the fossil-fuel phase-out, and the White House firing its cyber chief. It carries its own 5x7 bitmap font (stored as integers) and PNG encoder, draws flat-colour pictograms for every story, and writes a deterministic 1024x1024 image — two runs produce byte-identical output.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
